# Concept map: inbound marketing and CRM

Publicly described concepts, written in our own words. Use them to generate original
material. When the learner provides the official topic list, align with it and flag any
topic not covered here.

## 1. Inbound foundations

| Concept | Core idea | Common confusion |
|---|---|---|
| Inbound vs outbound | Attract people with useful content and experiences they look for, rather than interrupting them | Inbound does not mean "no paid media": paid can support inbound content |
| Inbound methodology: attract, engage, delight | Three ways of interacting with people: draw the right people in, help them find solutions and buy, keep helping after the purchase | These are not departments; marketing, sales and service act in all three |
| Flywheel | A circular model where satisfied customers feed growth; growth depends on the **force** added (what makes customers successful) and the **friction** removed (what slows them) | Not just "the funnel drawn as a circle": customers are an output *and* an input |
| Buyer persona | Semi-fictional representation of the ideal customer, based on research | Not a demographic segment; built on goals, challenges, behaviours |
| Buyer's journey | Awareness (the person notices a problem), consideration (defines it and explores solutions), decision (chooses a solution and a provider) | The journey is the buyer's process, not the company's sales process |

## 2. Content and attraction

| Concept | Core idea |
|---|---|
| Content strategy | Content mapped to personas and journey stages, with goals and a calendar |
| Pillar page and topic cluster | A broad page on a core topic linked to and from detailed pages on subtopics; organises a site around topics rather than isolated keywords |
| SEO basics | Search intent, on-page optimisation, technical health, links, user experience |
| Social media | Listening, publishing, engaging; choice of networks by persona |
| Content repurposing | One substantial piece turned into several formats |

## 3. Conversion

| Concept | Core idea |
|---|---|
| Conversion path | Call to action → landing page → form → thank-you page → follow-up e-mail |
| Offer / lead magnet | Content or service valuable enough to exchange contact details |
| Form strategy | Ask only what you need now; progressive profiling for the next conversions |
| Landing page best practices | One goal, clear value, no distracting navigation, proof |

## 4. E-mail and nurturing

| Concept | Core idea |
|---|---|
| Segmentation | Send relevant messages based on properties and behaviour |
| Lead nurturing | Sequence of helpful messages that moves a contact through the journey |
| Deliverability | Permission, list hygiene, engagement, authentication; avoid buying lists |
| Consent (GDPR) | Lawful basis, explicit opt-in where required, easy unsubscribe |
| Testing | A/B test one variable at a time on a meaningful sample |

## 5. CRM concepts

| Concept | Core idea |
|---|---|
| CRM objects | Records of different types, e.g. contacts (people), companies, deals (opportunities), tickets (service requests), linked by associations |
| Properties | Fields that store information on records; default and custom |
| Lifecycle stages | Where a contact stands in the relationship, typically from subscriber, lead, marketing qualified lead (MQL), sales qualified lead (SQL), opportunity, to customer and evangelist; stages should move forward based on clear rules |
| Lead scoring | Points for fit (who they are) and engagement (what they do) to prioritise follow-up |
| Pipelines and deal stages | Sequence of sales steps with probabilities; one pipeline per sales process |
| Lists / segments | Groups of records based on criteria; dynamic (update automatically) or static (fixed) |
| Workflows / automation | Rules triggered by enrolment criteria that perform actions (send e-mail, update property, create task) |
| Marketing-sales agreement (SLA) | Shared definitions (MQL, SQL) and commitments (follow-up times, volumes) |
| Service and delight | Tickets, knowledge base, feedback surveys (NPS, CSAT, CES) |

## 6. Measurement

| Concept | Core idea |
|---|---|
| Funnel and flywheel metrics | Visits, conversion rates, leads, MQLs, SQLs, customers, retention, referrals |
| Attribution | How credit for a conversion is shared across touchpoints (first touch, last touch, multi-touch) |
| Dashboards | Few indicators, linked to objectives, reviewed regularly |
| SMART goals | Specific, measurable, attainable, relevant, time-bound |
