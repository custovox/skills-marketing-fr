# Product page audit grid

Each criterion: 0 = absent or wrong, 1 = partial, 2 = good. Axis score = sum of points /
maximum x 100. Overall score = weighted average of the axes (default weights below; adapt
and state them if the page has a specific job, e.g. SEO entry page).

## A. Content (weight 30 %)

| # | Criterion | 2 = good when... |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Product title | Says what it is, for whom/what need, key variant; no keyword stuffing |
| A2 | Benefit-led summary | 3-5 bullet points above the fold, benefits before features |
| A3 | Detailed description | Answers the target's questions: what, how, why it works, for whom (and not for whom) |
| A4 | Usage instructions | How, when, how much, how long it lasts |
| A5 | Specifications / composition | Complete and readable (dimensions, materials, full ingredient list...) |
| A6 | Visuals | Several angles, scale, texture/in-use shots, zoom; consistent quality |
| A7 | Video or demonstration | Shows use or result when relevant |
| A8 | Tone and brand | Consistent with the brand promise and the persona |

## B. Reassurance (weight 25 %)

| # | Criterion | 2 = good when... |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Customer reviews | Visible near the title, enough volume, recent, negative ones visible and answered |
| B2 | Questions and answers / FAQ | Covers recurring objections found in reviews and tickets |
| B3 | Delivery information | Realistic dates (ideally "delivered by..."), costs, free-shipping threshold, near the button |
| B4 | Returns and guarantees | Clear conditions, link to the policy |
| B5 | Proof of claims | Labels, certifications, tests: each claim is supported and verifiable |
| B6 | Contact and help | Easy access to advice (chat, phone, e-mail) with response times |
| B7 | Payment security | Accepted means and security shown at the right moment |

Customer reviews have a measurable effect on sales (Chevalier & Mayzlin, 2006); a
meta-analysis of electronic word of mouth confirms a positive overall link with sales,
which varies with platform, product and metric (Babić Rosario et al., 2016).

## C. SEO (weight 20 %)

| # | Criterion | 2 = good when... |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Title tag and meta description | Unique, explicit, right length, matches search intent |
| C2 | H1 and heading structure | One H1 = product name; logical H2/H3 |
| C3 | URL | Short, readable, stable |
| C4 | Unique content | Not copied from the supplier or other pages |
| C5 | Structured data | Product markup with offer, availability, reviews (check with the Rich Results Test) |
| C6 | Images | Descriptive file names and alt texts, compressed |
| C7 | Internal links | Category, related products, guides |
| C8 | Performance and mobile | To check with PageSpeed Insights (Core Web Vitals) |

## D. Conversion (weight 25 %)

| # | Criterion | 2 = good when... |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Price clarity | Price, unit price, discounts and fees clear; no surprise at checkout |
| D2 | Call to action | Visible above the fold on mobile, explicit, sticky on long pages |
| D3 | Variants and stock | Easy selection, stock and delivery date per variant |
| D4 | Cross-sell and routines | Relevant complements, not intrusive |
| D5 | Friction | No forced account, no pop-up blocking the button, fast add to cart |
| D6 | Mobile ergonomics | Readable, tappable, image gallery usable with one hand |
| D7 | Accessibility | Contrast, alt texts, keyboard navigation, readable fonts |

## E. Sector axis (optional, weight taken from the others)

**Cosmetics (EU)**: full INCI list; claims compliant with the common criteria of
Commission Regulation (EU) No 655/2013 (legal compliance, truthfulness, evidential support,
honesty, fairness, informed decision-making); no medical claims; "hypoallergenic",
"natural", "clean" or environmental claims supported by evidence; precautions and allergens
visible; period after opening; reformulations clearly signalled.

**Food**: mandatory information, allergens, nutrition table, origin.
**Children's products / electrical goods**: safety warnings, age, standards, CE marking.

## Prioritisation

Impact (1-3) x ease (1-3, 3 = easy). Quick wins: impact 2-3 and ease 3. Group by owner so
each team leaves with its own list.

  electronic word of mouth on sales: a meta-analytic review of platform, product, and
  metric factors. *Journal of Marketing Research*, 53(3), 297-318.
  https://doi.org/10.1509/jmr.14.0380
- Chevalier, J. A., & Mayzlin, D. (2006). The effect of word of mouth on sales: online book
  reviews. *Journal of Marketing Research*, 43(3), 345-354.
  https://doi.org/10.1509/jmkr.43.3.345
- Commission Regulation (EU) No 655/2013 laying down common criteria for the justification
  of claims used in relation to cosmetic products.
- Free tools: Google PageSpeed Insights, Rich Results Test, Search Console; WAVE for
  accessibility.
