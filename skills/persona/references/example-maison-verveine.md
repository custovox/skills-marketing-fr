# Personas - Maison Verveine (natural cosmetics, D2C)

_October 2026 · Purpose: prepare the 2027 CRM plan and the response to the delivery and
quality issues observed since July 2026._

## 1. Sources

| ID | Source | Type | Size / sample | Date | Known biases |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Voice-of-the-Customer report (voc-skill) | qualitative, coded and counted | 205 verbatims (200 reviews, 5 calls) | Apr-Sep 2026 | unhappy and very happy customers over-represented |

## 2. Overview

| ID | Persona | One-line summary | Weight (S2) | Priority | Overall confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | The Committed Naturalist | Buys Maison Verveine *because* of what is (not) in the products and how they are made | 41 % of customers, 54 % of revenue | primary | High |
| P2 | The Results-Driven Skin Fixer | Hires a product to fix a specific skin problem and stays only if it works | 27 % of customers, 30 % of revenue | secondary | Medium |
| P3 | The Occasional Gift Buyer | Buys once or twice a year, mostly to give, on a deadline and with a code | 24 % of customers, 11 % of revenue | secondary | Medium |
| PN | The Marketplace Deal Hunter | Buys only on the marketplace during promotions, no brand attachment | 8 % of customers, 5 % of revenue | negative | Medium |

## 3. Persona cards

### P1 - The Committed Naturalist (primary)

> _"I want products I can trust, made by a brand that walks the talk."_ (illustrative)

| | Content | Sources | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Context | Customer for 2+ years; 3.4 orders a year on average; buys skincare, soap and solid shampoo | S2 | High |
| Goals / jobs to be done | Take care of her skin with short, plant-based formulas; reduce waste; feel consistent with her values | S1 (35 mentions of naturalness and transparency), S4 (4/6 interviews) | Medium |
| Triggers | Running out of a product (routine replenishment), newsletter launches | S2 (reorder every ~95 days), S3 (newsletter = 2nd traffic source) | High |
| Decision criteria | 1. Composition 2. Origin (France) 3. Packaging 4. Efficacy 5. Price | S4, S1 | Medium |
| Channels and touchpoints | Website on mobile, newsletter, app (early adopter), sometimes a partner organic store | S3, S2, S4 | High |
| Pains and barriers | Broken glass jars (waste + hassle), slower deliveries since July, no refill option | S1 (21 broken parcels, 33 delivery issues, 6 refill requests), S5 | High (delivery, breakage) / Low (refills) |
| Delights | Samples and handwritten note in the parcel; ingredient transparency | S1 (16 mentions), S4 | Medium |
| Objections | Plastic pumps feel inconsistent with the brand; price increases | S1, A | Low |
| Price sensitivity | Moderate: accepts premium prices if values and quality are there | S2 (low promo-code usage: 12 % of orders), S4 | Medium |

**In their words**
> « Composition courte et naturelle, ça me rassure. » - S1, AV-0035
>
> « Vous pourriez faire des écorecharges ? Pour une marque naturelle ce serait logique. » - S1, AV-0048

**What to do** - Tell the story behind formulas and suppliers; show the packaging roadmap
(refills, protective cardboard inserts instead of plastic); reward loyalty with early access
and samples rather than discounts; be transparent about the carrier problem.
**What to avoid** - Aggressive discounting (devalues the brand for her), greenwashing claims
that are not backed by facts, more plastic.

### P2 - The Results-Driven Skin Fixer (secondary)

> _"I don't care about the story if my skin doesn't improve."_ (illustrative)

| | Content | Sources | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Context | Arrives with a specific problem (dryness, sensitivity, uneven tone); first order is the Crème Nuit or the Sérum in 78 % of cases | S2 (first-order product), S4 (2/6 interviews) | High |
| Goals / jobs to be done | Visible results within a few weeks; comfort for sensitive skin | S1 (37 efficacy mentions), S4 | Medium |
| Triggers | A skin problem; reviews and social media recommendations | S3 (organic social + review sites = 31 % of first visits), S4 | Medium |
| Decision criteria | 1. Proven efficacy 2. Tolerance 3. Reviews 4. Texture 5. Price | S4, S1 | Medium |
| Channels and touchpoints | Reviews, social media, product pages; contacts customer service when something goes wrong | S3, S1 | Medium |
| Pains and barriers | Any change in formula; reactions to the reformulated Crème Nuit; not being able to reach customer service | S1 (4 skin-reaction reports in Sep, 36 service-access complaints), S5 | Medium |
| Delights | Visible results; texture | S1 | Medium |
| Objections | "Natural doesn't mean effective" | S4 (1/6), A | Low |
| Price sensitivity | Low if it works; churns fast if it does not | S2 (repeat rate 44 % after 2nd order vs 19 % after 1st), A | Medium |

**In their words**
> « Le sérum a vraiment unifié mon teint, je suis bluffée. » - S1, AV-0089
>
> « Ma peau a réagi à la Crème Nuit (rougeurs, picotements), ce n'était pas le cas avant. » - S1, AV-0174

**What to do** - Prove results (before/after protocols, dermatological testing, usage
guide); announce and explain any reformulation; follow up 3 weeks after purchase; give fast
access to an expert advisor.
**What to avoid** - Silent reformulations, generic "natural" messages without proof.

### P3 - The Occasional Gift Buyer (secondary)

> _"I need a nice gift that arrives on time."_ (illustrative)

| | Content | Sources | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Context | 1.2 orders a year, 58 % of them in November-December or around Mother's Day; buys the Coffret Découverte and hand creams | S2 | High |
| Goals / jobs to be done | Find a pleasant, "safe" gift with a premium feel | S2 (gift-wrap option ticked on 64 % of orders), S4 (1/6) | Medium |
| Triggers | Calendar events, promotional e-mails | S2, S3 | High |
| Decision criteria | 1. Delivery date 2. Presentation 3. Price with code 4. Brand image | S1, A | Low |
| Channels and touchpoints | Website on desktop, promo e-mails, gift guides | S3 | Medium |
| Pains and barriers | Late deliveries before an event; promo codes refused on gift boxes | S1 (AV-0182, call 5; 8 promo-code complaints) | Medium |
| Delights | Gift-ready packaging; handwritten note | S1, A | Low |
| Objections | Shipping costs below €59 | S1 (15 price/shipping mentions) | Medium |
| Price sensitivity | High: 57 % of their orders use a code | S2 | High |

**In their words**
> « Commande passée pour un anniversaire, arrivée après la date. » - S1, AV-0182
>
> « Par contre code de bienvenue refusé au paiement, j'ai payé plein tarif. » - S1, AV-0027

**What to do** - Guaranteed delivery dates with a last order day per event; clear code
conditions; gift guides; turn the gift recipient into a customer (insert card with an offer).
**What to avoid** - Promising dates the carrier cannot meet; codes with hidden exclusions.

### PN - The Marketplace Deal Hunter (negative persona)

| | Content | Sources | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Behaviour | Buys only on the marketplace, only during promotions; 0.9 orders a year; highest return rate (9 %) | S2 | High |
| Relationship with the brand | Little or none; compares prices across brands | S1 (marketplace reviews focus on price), A | Medium |

> « Les formats sont petits pour le prix. » - S1, AV-0010 (marketplace)

**Why it is a negative persona** - Low margin, high returns, no loyalty. Do not design
campaigns for this profile; keep the marketplace as a discovery channel and try to move
buyers to the website.

## 4. Comparison grid

| Variable | P1 Committed Naturalist | P2 Skin Fixer | P3 Gift Buyer | PN Deal Hunter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Main motivation | Values, composition | Visible results | A gift that pleases | The lowest price |
| Purchase trigger | Routine replenishment | Skin problem, reviews | Calendar event | Promotion |
| Price sensitivity | Moderate | Low (if it works) | High | Very high |
| Preferred channel | Mobile site, app, newsletter | Reviews, social, product pages | Desktop site, promo e-mails | Marketplace |
| Relationship with the brand | Strong, demanding | Conditional on results | Occasional | None |
| Main barrier | Waste, broken jars, delivery | Tolerance, reformulation | Delivery date, codes | Price |

## 5. Validation plan

| Priority | Persona | Attribute to validate | Current confidence | Method | Cost / time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P2 | Extent of skin reactions to the new Crème Nuit formula | Medium (4 reports) | Cosmetovigilance review + call the customers who bought since August | Low / 1 week |
| 2 | P1 | Willingness to buy refills, and at what price | Low | 5-question survey to the "loyal" RFM segment + pilot pre-order page | Low / 3 weeks |
| 3 | P3 | Decision criteria ranking (date vs price vs presentation) | Low | Post-purchase survey on gift orders in November | Low / 1 month |
| 4 | P2 | Share of first visits driven by reviews | Medium | Attribution analysis in analytics + "how did you hear of us" field | Low / 2 weeks |
| 5 | All | Personas' shares in partner stores | Not measured | Loyalty card or QR code in stores | Medium / 3 months |
