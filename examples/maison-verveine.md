# Maison Verveine: the fictional brand used across the pack

> **Entirely fictional.** Maison Verveine, its products, figures, customers and
> sources are invented for teaching purposes. Any resemblance to a real company is
> coincidental. Every example in this pack uses this brand so that the skills can be
> read as one continuous case: personas → customer journey → CRM plan.

## Identity

| | |
|---|---|
| Sector | Natural cosmetics (skincare, body care, solid haircare) |
| Model | Direct-to-consumer e-commerce (website + app), plus a marketplace shop and 40 partner organic stores |
| Founded | 2019, in the Drôme region (France) |
| Promise | "Short formulas, French plants, visible results" |
| Signature ingredient | Lemon verbena (verveine) |
| Team | 28 people, including a 5-person customer service team |

## Key figures (internal, fictional, year to September 2026)

- 38,400 active customers (at least one order in the last 12 months)
- Average order value: €42; free shipping threshold raised from €49 to €59 in June 2026
- Repeat purchase rate within 12 months: 31 %
- 62 % of orders on mobile; app launched in 2025 (9 % of orders)
- Newsletter: 52,000 subscribers, 34 % open rate
- Carrier change in July 2026 to cut costs; delivery times and complaints rose afterwards
